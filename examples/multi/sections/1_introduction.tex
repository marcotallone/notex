Welcome to NoTeX~\cite{github}, a modern \LaTeX\ template designed to make your note-taking and document creation experience enjoyable and efficient.
Whether you're writing notes for your next project, the documentation for your code, or just enjoy the look of this template, NoTeX has you covered.

The idea behind this template is to take care of all the formatting, aesthetics, and structure for you, so you can focus on the content itself and organize your notes in a clean and visually appealing way.
NoTeX comes with a set of pre-defined styles, useful commands and commonly used macros to help you create professional-looking documents with minimal effort.

The template is designed to be modular, allowing you to easily include or exclude components as needed, and customizable, so you can adapt it to your own style and preferences.

\vspace{1em}

Aside from the LaTeX template itself, NoTeX also provides a fast \gls{CLI} companion utility written in \cc{C++} to manage template installation, project initialization, document compilation, and more\dots\
This can be optionally installed on your system to streamline your workflow and make it even easier to use NoTeX.

The following sections in this document will guide you through the usage of the framework, from its installation to the creation and management of your own projects with NoTeX, showcasing the various features and capabilities of this template.
